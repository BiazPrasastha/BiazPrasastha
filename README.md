## About

I'm a full stack engineer in Bandung. At PT. Gistex I build ERP applications and connect textile machines (dyeing, jet relaxing, knitting, weighing, defect detection) to business systems over MQTT and Rest API, as part of the move from SAP to Datatex NOW.

Before that, from 2024 to 2026, I worked on Healmity, an EMR used in 250+ clinics, and WaitHub, a queue platform that has processed 10M+ transactions. Most of my backend work there was Rust (Axum).

On the side I take freelance projects, mostly academic, publishing, and university platforms. I'm also studying for a B.Sc. in Computer Science.
